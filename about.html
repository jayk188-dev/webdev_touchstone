About Us

Our Story

Our mission has always been to provide wholesome and fresh food to our friends, family, and neighbors. What started out of our kitchen with the support of our community has grown to a place where we hope everyone feels welcome and leaves well fed.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe in empowering our community and making sure ingredients don't travel further than you do. Sourced from our local partners, all of our wheat and rye are sourced from nearby grain mills.
